Refund Policy

1. General Policy

At Dream's Academy, we ensure the highest quality of education. We want our students to be fully satisfied. However, due to the digital nature of our courses and services, we have a strict policy regarding refunds.

2. Eligibility for Refund

Refunds will only be considered under the following circumstances:

  • If you made duplicate payments accidentally for the exact same course.
  • If the course content does not match the curriculum provided before enrollment.

3. Non-refundable Conditions

  • No refund will be issued if you change your mind after purchasing.
  • No refund will be provided if you fail to complete the course or understand the topic due to a lack of effort.
  • Digital services, tools, and proxies bought via our platform are completely non-refundable.

4. Processing Time

Approved refunds may take 7-10 working days to be reflected in your original payment method.
